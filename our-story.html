Our story

Our origin story starts with four passionate University of Alberta science graduates, all convinced that connecting research to practice was possible.

Layout only. This page keeps the structure and wording of the current site; the redesign concentrates on the home page and What We Do.

Matthew Pyper — Principal

In 2007, Matthew recognized a need to bridge the gap between researchers and decision-makers and has been pursuing this passion ever since. After the Sustainable Forest Management Network, he built a knowledge exchange program in the Department of Renewable Resources at the University of Alberta.

Shelagh Pyper — Principal

An educator in spirit her whole life: the demonstration stage at the Telus World of Science, a hands-on math and science curriculum, and the University of Alberta's engineering outreach in northern communities. Founding Fuse broadened that work to nationwide programs.

Sonya Odsen — Lead Ecologist and Science Communicator

From her first research notes and a caribou sketch, Sonya has always looked for new ways to convey the significance of science to diverse audiences.

Kate Broadley — Director of Knowledge Mobilization

From public education at the Bamfield Marine Sciences Centre and Alberta Parks to helping scientists overcome their communication challenges.
