Fuse transitioning interpretive signage work to Fenwood Creative

Fuse newsSeptember 3, 20251 min read
By Fuse Consulting
All articles

Owned and led by our former Director of Education and Outreach, Emma Ausford, Fenwood Creative will carry forward the creativity, spirit, and science-based storytelling you’ve come to know and love from Fuse’s interpretive signage work.

With years of experience leading interpretive projects at Fuse, Emma is excited to continue this work. Fenwood Creative will focus on crafting inspiring interpretive signs and exhibits that connect people to the natural world through beautiful design, thoughtful storytelling, and a deep love for the land.

Fuse will continue to be your go-to partner for:

  • Infographics

  • Knowledge synthesis

  • Practical guides

  • Facilitation

  • knowledge exchange partnerships

  • Education & outreach about conservation and land management projects

  • and other science communication products and services.

We feel this decision will enable the interpretive signage work to continue to grow, while maintaining the focus and impact of our other work in connecting knowledge to practice.

Visit www.fenwoodcreative.ca to learn more and follow @fenwoodcreative on Instagram to stay connected.

And if you aren’t following Fuse already, be sure to follow us on LinkedIn and @fuseconsulting on Instagram.
