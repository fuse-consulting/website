Workshops & Facilitation

Northern restoration community of practice workshop

SERN BC · Prince George, BC · 2024

Context and objectives

SERN BC asked Fuse to help with a two-day workshop in Prince George.

Why the project was done

The goal was to spark a community of practice for restoration professionals in Northern BC.

What we did

Fuse worked with the planning committee on an agenda that combined presentations of practical insights with collaborative breakout sessions, designed a facilitation plan to focus the conversation and capture ideas, and managed all event logistics. Attendees voted on the most important next steps.

Results and impact

SERN BC expressed the desire to keep building the community of practice, and Fuse developed a proposal of next steps from the feedback. Post-workshop survey: "honestly - best facilitation I have ever seen."

Deliverables

  • Project management: logistics, registration, presentations
  • Facilitation with group work and keynote speakers
  • 'What We Heard' report
  • Proposal of next steps for the community of practice

Wildfire Community Impact Research workshop

FPInnovations · Canada · 2024

Context and objectives

FPInnovations and partners set out to create a Canadian framework for wildfire community impact research. The workshop was the second Canadian gathering of researchers in this field, and brought in wildfire agencies from outside Canada.

Why the project was done

Agencies have studied how wildfires affect communities for almost 100 years without international collaboration; the workshop aimed to foster an international community of practice.

What we did

Fuse planned and facilitated the 4-hour hybrid workshop, providing format and equipment so 25 people from over a dozen agencies on Zoom could work with 35 people in the room across more than seven time zones. Fuse harvested information from presentations and breakouts.

Deliverables

  • Pre-reading package
  • Process map (digital scrolling version and stand-up banner)
  • Posters and editable whiteboard graphics for breakouts
  • 'What We Heard' report

"Sacred Connection to Mother Earth" documentary

Government of Canada – Canadian Forest Service (Northern Forestry Centre, Land Reclamation & Ecosystem Restoration Lab) · Edmonton region, Alberta · 2023

Context and objectives

In early 2022 the Canadian Forest Service's Land Reclamation & Ecosystem Restoration Lab contracted Fuse to support community outreach about land reclamation. With Elder-in-Residence Heather Poitras and Kôhkom Kathy Hamelin, the film explores how three groups of Alberta women (Elders, scientists and farmers) connect with the land.

Why the project was done

The goal is to showcase different perspectives and support students in building authentic, positive relationships with the land and their community. The intention was to go beyond standard truth and reconciliation protocol and build trust and authentic relationships.

What we did

Everyone leading the project was a woman or identified as two-spirited, and Fuse committed to hiring as many Indigenous contractors as possible, including in pre- and post-production.

Results and impact

The film won the 2024 Alberta Film and Television Award (Rosie) for Best Documentary under 30 minutes. It premiered at the Royal Alberta Museum during Canadian Forestry Week and was screened at the Telus World of Science with a panel Q&A.

Deliverables

  • 17-minute film for Grades 4–9, in English and French
  • Worksheets and custom illustrations for Alberta teachers

Learning from Lake Sturgeon zine

Wildlife Conservation Society (WCS) Canada · Kit Aski Nahnuun (Moose Cree Homeland, Northern Ontario) · 2023, 2025

Context and objectives

In 2023 a WCS Canada scientist asked Fuse to develop a series of science communication pieces, including a zine about the Learning from Lake Sturgeon program for the Moose Cree community. In 2025 WCS Canada's Ontario Northern Boreal Landscape Program came back for an updated issue.

Flow device best management practices

Project Beaver (formerly The Beaver Coalition) · Oregon, US · 2022–2023

Context and objectives

The Beaver Coalition (now Project Beaver), an ENGO, asked Fuse to design and run a collaborative review of best management practices for installing flow devices that allow coexistence with beavers and meet Oregon's fish passage bylaws. The collaboration spanned national and state agencies (ODFW, NOAA, ODSL, USFWS) and NGOs.

Why the project was done

Releasing the BMP was a key step in balancing the needs of beavers and salmonids in Oregon's waterways; the conversation had been stalled for two years before Fuse was hired.

What we did

Fuse facilitated multi-sector meetings, held one-on-one conversations with each organization between meetings, summarized each party's needs and concerns, and coordinated the review. Fuse also synthesized and illustrated the BMP with conceptual diagrams, permitting flow charts and tables.

Results and impact

Within 12 months, the work enabled the release and roll-out of the BMP.

Deliverables

  • Best Management Practices for Pond Levelers and Culvert Protection Systems (guide)
  • Custom illustrations and decision diagrams
  • Related factsheet series

Wildfire information system user engagement

Canadian Forest Service · Canada · 2022–2023

Context and objectives

The Canadian Wildland Fire Information System (CWFIS) monitors fire danger and fire occurrence across Canada and produces daily fire weather and fire behaviour maps used by many users.

Why the project was done

Ahead of launching a new CWFIS website in 2023, the client wanted detailed feedback on how the site was used, what wildland fire information people need and how the system could serve users better.

What we did

In consultation with CWFIS, Fuse coordinated and facilitated eight focus groups with a list of users and user groups provided by the client.

Results and impact

The 'What We Heard' report was used to decide which changes to implement on the new version of the website.

Deliverables

  • Eight focus groups
  • Record of sessions with participant contacts and raw and summarized feedback
  • 'What We Heard' report with recommendations and a ranking of priorities

Restoration framework for legacy seismic lines

Government of Alberta · Alberta · 2021

Context and objectives

In 2021 the Government of Alberta asked Fuse to help update the Provincial Restoration and Establishment Framework for Legacy Seismic Lines. Matthew Pyper and Sonya Odsen were lead authors, in collaboration with Woodlands North.

Why the project was done

The framework aimed to set targets and expectations for restoration work, including criteria to evaluate mid-term outcomes and forecast recovery trajectories.

What we did

Fuse worked with the province to identify the framework's strategic goals and desired outcomes, interviewed key government members and independent contractors, and engaged industry stakeholders and Indigenous communities early in development.

Deliverables

  • Restoration framework covering key steps of the restoration life cycle
  • Visual guide to support implementation of restoration programs

Wildfire Resilience Consortium strategic advising

FPInnovations and Institute for Catastrophic Loss Reduction · Canada

Context and objectives

The Wildfire Resilience Consortium of Canada (WRCC) aims to become a national Centre of Excellence focused on the uptake of technology innovations, knowledge mobilization, and Indigenous fire science and cultural use of fire.

Why the project was done

The client asked Fuse for strategic advising throughout the WRCC's visioning, partnership development and funding application stage.

What we did

During the 'Engage' phase, Fuse provided strategic advising, facilitation, project management, writing and graphic design, reaching out across the wildland fire ecosystem. Using the Chaordic Stepping Stones, Shelagh Pyper guided foundational partners to a shared vision, a proposed structure and Principles of Working Together.

Results and impact

Foundational and supportive partners were established during this phase, and Fuse went on to support the 'Connect' phase.

Deliverables

  • Centre of Excellence application
  • Shared vision and proposed structure
  • Principles of Working Together
  • Invitation to supporters and partner members
