Workshops &Facilitation

Relationships are critical for making difficult decisions in conservation and land use management. We help the conversation move.

Talk to us about a session →Is this for you? ↓

Why contact us

You’ll want us if…

Fuse is regularly called on for skill, expertise and tact to help navigate conversations, and to plan and run workshops and field tours, in person or virtual.

Who we do this for
  • Research programs
  • Multi-sector partnerships
  • Government and industry
  • Indigenous communities
  • Communities of practice
  • You need people together to share knowledge, gather input or work toward a specific outcome.
  • A difficult conversation needs a facilitator who knows the science.
  • Your team wants to communicate its research to non-specialists, for the long term.
  • Your audience is spread across a large area.
  • You are about to finalize something public-facing and want genuine feedback first.
  • The issue is best understood on the ground.

Our goal is to get people engaged, and to make the most of the time they invested to attend.

We draw on techniques that focus on interaction and engagement, as opposed to listening to presentation after presentation. Matthew Pyper is trained in the methods of the Art of Hosting.
