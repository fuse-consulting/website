Workshops &Facilitation

Relationships are critical for making difficult decisions in conservation and land use management. We help the conversation move.

When facilitation can help

Fuse is regularly called on for skill, expertise and tact to help navigate conversations, and to plan and run workshops and field tours, in person or online.

We work with research programs, multi-sector partnerships, government and industry, Indigenous communities and communities of practice.

Signs you may need a facilitator

  • You need people together to share knowledge, gather input or work toward a specific outcome.
  • A difficult conversation needs a facilitator who knows the science.
  • Your team wants to communicate its research to non-specialists, for the long term.
  • Your audience is spread across a large area.
  • You are about to finalize something public-facing and want genuine feedback first.
  • The issue is best understood on the ground.

Our goal is to get people engaged, and to make the most of the time they invested to attend.

We draw on techniques that focus on interaction and engagement, as opposed to listening to presentation after presentation. Matthew Pyper is trained in the methods of the Art of Hosting.

Featured case study

Silviculture Innovation Program facilitation

Bulkley Valley Research Centre (BVRC) · British Columbia · 2023

Context and objectives

In response to the BC government's recommendations for old-growth forest management, the Bulkley Valley Research Centre enlisted Fuse to develop the Silviculture Innovation Program (SIP). The program aims to advance innovative silviculture systems through research and education, improve access to forest management information, engage practitioners and inform policy.

Why the project was done

The program needed a clear vision and priorities during its conceptualization phase.

What we did

Fuse hosted a knowledge summit to identify knowledge gaps and investment priorities, with live graphic recording, and organized an in-person retreat where stakeholders shared their vision for the SIP. Fuse continues to facilitate meetings of the strategic action group.

Deliverables

  • Workshop facilitation
  • Conflict management
  • Live graphic recording
  • Agenda development
  • Workshop reports
  • Progress reports and project briefs
  • Webinar facilitation

Formats we facilitate

  • WorkshopsA facilitated, interactive session that brings people together to share knowledge, explore ideas, solve problems or work toward a shared goal.
  • Graphic RecordingLive visual note-taking during a meeting, produced in real time on a large visual surface.
  • Science Communication TrainingTraining that builds your team’s skills in communicating science to non-specialist audiences.
  • WebinarAn online presentation or discussion, sometimes recorded and shared afterwards.
  • Field ToursA guided, in-person visit to a physical site, led by an expert.
  • Community EngagementStructured processes that involve local communities in decisions, or in the co-creation of knowledge.
  • Focus GroupsSmall, structured group discussions that gather targeted qualitative feedback.
